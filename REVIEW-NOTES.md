# Ad keywords workspace: overview

The Ad Keywords workspace helps the team plan Google Ads campaigns from real search data. Starting from a store collection, product or category, it finds the searches that already bring people to the site (from Search Console or an imported report). It then proposes keywords and headlines grounded in those searches.

Searches that come from real data are kept apart from AI suggestions, and AI suggestions must point back to the searches they came from. Queries that don't fit the target are left out automatically but stay visible for review. When the data is incomplete or stale, generation stops instead of guessing.

Detailed design notes are kept privately. The security overview is in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
